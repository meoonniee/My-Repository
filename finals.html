Destination Overview

Plaridel (formerly Quingua) in the province of Bulacan

Plaridel is a peaceful municipality in Bulacan that is easy to reach from Metro Manila. It is ideal for a day trip or weekend walk centered on centuries-old local heritage and relaxing riverside scenery.

Best Time to Visit Dec – Feb
Average Daily Budget ₱500 – ₱1,000
Travel Time from Manila 1 to 1.5 hours
Main Vibe Heritage & Nature

Sample Itinerary

Choose a 1-day trip or a 2-day weekend

Morning

Visit Plaridel Church and Simborio Chapel while the weather is cool.

Afternoon

Take a slow stroll around the old town plaza and rest in the shade.

Evening

Enjoy sunset views near the Angat River area before heading home.

Day 1: Plaridel Heritage Walk

Explore Plaridel Church, Simborio Chapel, and nearby historical markers around Poblacion.

Day 2: Nature & Neighboring Towns

Spend a quiet morning by the riverside and green outskirts, then visit nearby Malolos or Bustos.

Travel Tips

Helpful reminders for your trip

How to Get There

Ride a bus or van bound for Bulacan, or drive north via NLEX.

Local Transport

Use tricycles or jeepneys around town. Bring coins and small bills.

Packing & Etiquette

Wear comfy shoes, dress modestly in churches, and ask before taking photos of people.
